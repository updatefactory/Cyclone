    Sea areas

    Rough to very rough seas

    East-central and west-central Bay of Bengal along the track. Fishermen should stay out of the sea until the system has passed.

    What to do now

    Landfall is about three days away. This is the time to prepare, not wait.

    Before landfall

    • Check IMD and district administration alerts every few hours.
    • Charge phones and power banks; keep a torch and radio ready.
    • Store drinking water, dry food and essential medicines.
    • Secure loose roofing, signboards and outdoor items.
    • Keep documents in a waterproof bag.

    If you live on the coast

    • Know your nearest cyclone shelter and route.
    • Move out early when authorities ask for evacuation.
    • Boats should be brought ashore and tied down.
    • Stay away from beaches, rivers and low-lying areas.
    • After landfall, avoid fallen power lines and flooded roads.
